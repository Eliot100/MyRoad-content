## Stages (estimate hours; label as estimate)

### Stage 1: Foundations — maps, atlas and space (estimate 15–25h)
- **Goal**: Map and atlas skills (scale, coordinates, layers, cross-sections), location factors, basic human-environment links.
- **Target level**: Entry.
- **Topics/tiles**: map reading; reading the approved atlases; climate and population basics. Kind: understanding.
- **Finish criteria**: Correctly interpret a simple map and identify 2 human-environment links.

### Stage 2: Geography of Eretz Israel (estimate 25–35h)
- **Goal**: Physical and human geography of Israel, using the 1:250,000 map and the geological map (both allowed in the 57381 exam).
- **Target level**: Intermediate.
- **Topics/tiles**: regions, landforms, water, settlement and development. Kind: understanding + review.
- **Finish criteria**: Answer a map-based question about one region using two map sources.

### Stage 3: Geography of the Middle East (estimate 20–30h)
- **Goal**: Dynamics of the region (resources, population, space and conflict) and implications for Israel.
- **Target level**: Intermediate.
- **Topics/tiles**: Kind: understanding + review.
- **Finish criteria**: Analyze one current-style issue using map + text; state one implication for Israel.

### Stage 4: נתי"ב — analysis of basic phenomena in space (estimate 20–30h)
- **Goal**: Apply core geographic concepts and processes to unfamiliar spaces.
- **Target level**: Bagrut level.
- **Finish criteria**: Explain one spatial process with one supporting geographic factor.

### Stage 5: Specialization unit (estimate 25–35h)
- **Goal**: One of כדור הארץ והסביבה / הפיתוח והתכנון המרחבי (the two chapters in the summer תשפ"ו 57282 paper). Sustainability and natural-disaster topics fit here.
- **Target level**: Bagrut level.
- **Finish criteria**: Compare two approaches to a sustainability or disaster challenge, supported by one geographic factor.

### Stage 6: Integrated review and mock (estimate 15–20h)
- **Goal**: Mixed practice (maps, graphs, short analysis) under time; 57381 summer תשפ"ו was 3 hours, 57282 was 2 hours.
- **Finish criteria**: Complete a full-style mock with ≥70% (self-scored).

## Level-up rules (3 levels per stage)
- Level 1: Identification from map or text.
- Level 2: Explanation of a process or link.
- Level 3: Evaluation or comparison of options.
- Advance on consistent Level 2.
- Spacing: review tiles every 4–7 days; map-skill retrieval mixed with content.

## 3 ORIGINAL sample items
1. **Map skill**: במפה של ישראל מופיע קו אורך 35°E וקו רוחב 32°N. איזה אזור משוער מתואר? (a) צפון הארץ (b) מרכז הארץ (c) דרום הארץ. (tests coordinate reading; check the answer against a real map before use).
2. **Human-environment**: הסבר בקצרה כיצד מיקומה של עיר חוף משפיע על כלכלתה ועל הסיכונים הסביבתיים שלה. (accept reasoned links).
3. **Sustainability**: השווה בין שתי דרכים להתמודד עם מחסור במים באזור צחיח: התפלה מול חיסכון ומיחזור. ציין יתרון אחד וחיסרון אחד לכל אחת.
